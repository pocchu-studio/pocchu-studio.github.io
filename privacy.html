Pocchu Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Pocchu Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Pocchu Studio to publish original animated videos to Pocchu Studio's own social media accounts (TikTok, the "Pocchu" Facebook Page and the Instagram account @anime.pocchu). The App is used only by Pocchu Studio and is not offered to the public.

2. Information the App accesses

When Pocchu Studio connects its own TikTok account, the App receives, with permission:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) — to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Account statistics and the list of our own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts — to measure how our videos perform.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos — to post our own videos.

When Pocchu Studio connects its own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account through Meta, the App receives, with permission:

  • The Page ID, Page name and an access token for our own Page, and the ID and username of the Instagram account linked to it — to post to the correct accounts.
  • Permission to publish videos and Reels to our own Page and Instagram account, and the insights (views, likes, comments, shares) of our own posts — to measure how our videos perform.

The App does not access information about other users, followers' personal data, messages, or contacts on any platform.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to post Pocchu Studio's videos and to review their performance so that we can improve future videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on Pocchu Studio's own computer, protected by the operating system's account security. They are not uploaded to any other server.

5. Retention and deletion (data deletion instructions)

Data is kept only while it is needed for the purposes above. The App stores no data about anyone other than Pocchu Studio's own accounts.

To remove the App's access and have its data deleted:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services > remove Pocchu Uploader.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings and privacy > Settings > Apps and websites (and Business integrations on Instagram) > remove Pocchu Uploader.

After access is removed, the stored tokens and statistics are deleted from Pocchu Studio's computer. You can also request deletion by email to the contact address below; we will confirm deletion within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not collect information from them.

7. Changes and contact
